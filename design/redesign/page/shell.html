Her panel

  • The left panel is 148×360: the webcam view to y 266, her UI under it.
  • The webcam view is the prototype's, unchanged in design: its room, door, corridor, him and her. Each state is baked from the prototype's own screenshot (exactly 2× the grid): down 2× in linear light, then ordered-dithered to the curated palettes.
  • His room and him: the desk's Graphite (near-black wall and floor, the slate door, the chair, the silhouette, the glowing glasses). What shows through the door and the daydream: the PC-98 set plus the desk's darks, so the prototype's colours stay close. The light under the door is a gold bar.
  • Live on top: the headset icon lights cyan with the headset on; his glasses go grey-green under Excel.
  • UI: beige paper windows with plum and purple bevels, pink buttons, rose hearts, Tiny5.

The dog

  • He jumps up on your lap: chest up, paws on the desk, covering the keys he likes best (Q W E, A S D).
  • A shiba in the desk's palette (copper fur, cream cheeks, brows and chest), painted with fixed values so the desk's uneven light can't wash him out.
  • Stroke his head with the mouse (gently: too fast does not count). The bar fills in his copper; at full he hops off. Petted: eyes closed, ears flat, hearts.

Desk + monitor

  • The right screen is 492×360 at x 148: bezel 484×260 at 4, 3 (5 px), game screen at 9, 8, taskbar 474×15 at 9, 241, desk from y 263.
  • A dark room lit almost only by the monitor. Its light is sampled from the screen every few frames, so the desk goes blue in the crypt, warm in town, cream under Excel.
  • Real life stays out of the RPG's frames: the PC is Windows 98 (the taskbar: Start, the two task buttons with the Tab hint on the one behind, Sleep, the tray with the wallet and the clock), iOS on the phone, flat glass cards for YOU and the feed. Text is Tiny5 only; the note is Tiny5 sheared into a ballpoint slant.
  • One camera for the desk: eye level at the monitor's top edge, depth lines meeting at x 246, y 3. The keyboard is built from 3D caps (sculpted tops, front skirts, the sides that face the centre) on a case with a typing angle; the phone lies flat and turned, its call screen placed on the glass with unscaled glyphs.
  • Class colours tie the screen to the keyboard: each portrait has a 1 px outline in its hero's colour, and his keys glow in it (1 2 3 knight steel, Z X C Garrote ember, Q W E mage violet, A S D priest gold). Ember is Moonwell's copper; on the desk it is a UI-only slot, so it never enters the dither. Tab Excel green, H Zone cyan, M moss.
  • The Goblin Fuel button glows gold (the next action) from the yawn mark. The Zone aura lights the bezel rim cyan and spills on the desk.

Grid

  • Internal resolution 640×360: ×2 = 1280×720 base, ×3 = 1080p, ×4 = 1440p, ×6 = 4K.
  • Game screen 474×232 (465 snaps to 464 CSS).
  • Battle area 334 wide: band 64, picture 142, skill bar 26.
  • Side panel 140×232.
  • Smallest text: Tiny5 at 8 px, caps 5 px = 10 CSS px.

Colour meanings

  • Gold: money, prices, the next action, combo.
  • Crimson: danger, enemy HP, debuffs, wind-ups and the skill that answers them.
  • Moss: party HP, buffs, healing.
  • Arcane violet: magic, the town portal.
  • Rarity: bone, moss, stone blue, arcane, copper.
  • Flow: yellow (its own UI-only colour), flashing moon white when it steps up.

Frames

  • UI surfaces run through the world's ramps and Bayer dither: bronze, leather and parchment have gradients and grain, and cast soft shadows on whatever is beneath.
  • Bronze plates are lit from the top left. Slots are recessed wells with an inner shadow and a lit lower lip, and rare items glow up from the slot floor.
  • Text, icons, rarity lines and state frames stay palette-exact. Dark text only sits on solid fills.
  • Portraits are lit like the scene: warm key from the upper right, cold rim from the left, candle glow behind.
  • Skill slots are 15×18 with a 13×13 icon on a class tile (knight iron, Garrote plum cloak, mage violet runes, priest gilded rays). Three per hero: two skills and the ultimate, whose slot is gilded (gold frame, corner studs, a crest with a gem over the rail, its key on a gold tab; it glows when ready, turns copper with a lock while locked). Party cards are 33 px wide, four across.
