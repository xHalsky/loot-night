The premise

When the sky passed judgment, iron angels came down burning and died where they fell. The world was rebuilt on their bodies. Ravenhold stands in the ribcage of one; under its crypts, something grows in the angel's body. You go down.

Every word stays medieval: saints, relics, iron. The RPG plays it straight. "Judgment" is also the weekly raid, so the boys' "judgment day at 11?" means something in the world.

The rules that hold

  • Iron is the stone family, darker and riveted.
  • The angels' eye-lights are crimson: enemies and danger, as before.
  • Gilding on buildings is umber, so gold still means loot.
  • Each depth owns one family of Moonwell 32: no new colours.
  • Ideas from the films, never their designs. At most one nod per screen.
  • The roster is a reskin: stats, roles, moves and the HUD don't change.
