# Outside playtest (the slice criteria, ROADMAP.md)

One person who has not seen the game, two nights, about 25 minutes. You watch; you do not explain.

## Before
- A laptop with sound, headphones off (they need to hear her). Open the published build, New game.
- Say only: "It's a birthday game for a friend. Play it like you would at home. I can't help you."

## While they play (tick or note the time)
| Criterion | What to watch | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Understands within 2 minutes | When do they first press Tab on footsteps by themselves? Do they read the card? | time: |
| Gets caught at least once | Each catch: what were they doing? | 1st catch at: |
| Their own fault | Right after the first catch ask: "Whose fault was that?" (game / me) | answer: |
| 3+ visit types over two nights | Tally: Peek / Snack / Chat / Cuddle. Did they answer each one differently? | P S C Cu |
| Wants another night | At the morning screen, before you say anything: do they click Next night? Then ask "One more?" | yes / no |

Also note: anything they said out loud, where they hovered or clicked and nothing happened, any moment
they looked lost for more than ~10 s, and whether they ever peeked, opened the plan, used the headset.

## After (2 minutes)
1. "What was the game about?"  2. "What told you she was coming?"  3. "What was the most annoying thing?"
4. "What was the best moment?"

## Report
Open an Issue titled "Playtest: <first name>, <date>" with the table, the notes and the answers, label
`balance`. The balance pass (#2) repeats until three testers in a row pass all five criteria.
