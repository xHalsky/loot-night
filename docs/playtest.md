# Outside playtest (the slice criteria, ROADMAP.md)

One person who has not seen the game, two nights (Monday and Tuesday), about 20 minutes. You watch; you do not explain.

## The link (close friends)

**https://xhalsky.github.io/loot-night/** (GitHub Pages, from the `playtest` branch; how a build gets there:
[tech.md](design/tech.md), Hosting). Send it with:

> Send the version number from the title screen with every bug report.

Saves live in the browser, tied to this address: the same browser on the same computer keeps the week going. The
address and the save key stay as they are for the whole playtest (changing either resets everyone's progress).

## Before
- A laptop with sound, headphones off (they need to hear her). Open the link above (the title says PLAYTEST
  BUILD and the version), Start Monday night. Saves from older builds are gone, so everyone starts on Monday.
- Say only: "It's a game I'm making. Play it like you would at home. I can't help you."
- F1 is the debug panel: don't open it in front of them.

## What the two nights hold
- **Monday:** the boys' chat on the phone (Billy, Mike and Lance, stoked for Age of Judgment), How to hide (three
  tiles), then tonight's three stars, her peeks and snack runs, Excel (Q4 pays dollars), the phone's Shop, the party of four. No Tiredness, no calls. The night ends at 01:00 (about 6 real minutes).
- **Tuesday:** Billy's note (lean in), How to hide with the headset and Lean in new, the night opens with her yoga
  (watch whether they hold the door and lean in; note if she spots them), her first visit is a chat (safe the first
  time), Tiredness, and the night can run past 01:00 to dawn (6 to 14 minutes). Pulled plug: Tuesday again.

## While they play (tick or note the time)
| Criterion | What to watch | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Understands within 2 minutes | When do they first press Tab on footsteps by themselves? Do they read How to hide, listen to Billy's note? | time: |
| Gets caught at least once | Each catch: what were they doing? | 1st catch at: |
| Their own fault | Right after the first catch ask: "Whose fault was that?" (game / me) | answer: |
| 3+ visit types over two nights | Tally: Peek / Snack run (Monday) / Chat (Tuesday). Did they answer each one differently? | P S C |
| Wants another night | At the morning screen, before you say anything: do they click Start Tuesday night? Then ask "One more?" | yes / no |

Also note: anything they said out loud, where they hovered or clicked and nothing happened, any moment
they looked lost for more than ~10 s, and whether they ever peeked under the door, opened the plan, worked in
Excel, picked up the phone (the Shop), used the headset, or slept at 23:00.

## After (2 minutes)
1. "What was the game about?"  2. "What told you she was coming?"  3. "What was the most annoying thing?"
4. "What was the best moment?"

## Report
Open an Issue titled "Playtest: <first name>, <date>" with the table, the notes and the answers, label
`balance`. The balance pass (#2) repeats until three testers in a row pass all five criteria.
